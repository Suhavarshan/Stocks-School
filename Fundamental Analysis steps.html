🚀Steps to Do Fundamental Analysis

1. Understand the Business

Learn what the company does, what products or services it offers, and how it makes money

2. Check Revenue

Look at whether the company's revenue is growing over time.

3. Check Profit

See whether the company is consistently making profits and whether those profits are improving.

4. Check Debt

Look at how much debt the company has compared with its financial strength.

5. Check EPS

EPS (Earnings Per Share) helps you understand how much profit is earned for each share.

6. Check P/E Ratio

Compare the company's P/E ratio with its own history and similar companies in the same industry.

7. Check Cash Flow

See whether the company's main business is generating healthy cash flow.

8. Understand the Risks

Consider competition, industry conditions, debt, business challenges, and other risks.

9. Make an Informed Decision

After studying all these factors, decide whether the company fits your investment goals and risk level.

Simple Formula :

Business → Revenue → Profit → Debt → EPS → P/E → Cash Flow → Risk → Decision
