About Me

Hello! I am Suhavarshan


I am a 16-year-old student with a passion for learning about the stock market and web development. I created this website to help beginners to understand the basics and fundamentals of the stock market in simple and easy-to-understand in both English and Tamil.


My goal is to make stock market education accessible to everyone, especially those who are just starting their learning journey. On this website, you'll find clear explanations of important concepts such as shares, investing, trading, risk, and other stock market fundamentals.

Thank you for visiting my website. I hope it helps you learn something new!
