Stock Market Basics:A Beginners Guide

What is Stock Marketing ?

A stock market is a place where people buy and sell shares of companies. When you buy one share of a company, you become a small owner of that company. If the company grows, the value of your share may increase. If the company performs poorly, your investment may lose value. Think of it like owning a very tiny piece of a large business

Why do People Invest ?

People invest because keeping money only in a savings account may not always beat inflation. Investing can help grow wealth over many years, but it also involves risk. Common goals include:

  • Retirement

  • Buying a house

  • Children's Education

  • Finacial Freedom

What Is Share ?

A Share represents small ownership in a company

Example:

Imagine a company has 100 shares. If you buy 5 shares, you own 5% of that company.

What is NSE and BSE?

India has two major stock exchanges.

  • NSE(National Stock Exchange)

  • BSE(Bombay Stock Exchange)

Companies list their shares on these exchanges so investors can buy and sell them.

What I Demat Account ?

A Demat account stores your shares electronically.

It works like a bank account, but instead of money, it holds your investments.

Trading vs Investing

Risks

Never invest money that you may need urgently.

Diversifying your investments and learning before investing can help manage risk, but it cannot eliminate it.
